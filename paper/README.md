# Paper

`categorical_rmt.tex` is the canonical source of the paper, with its bibliography `sample.bib`, the style file `JHEP.bst` and the figure files. `categorical_rmt.pdf` is compiled from them (77 pages). `environment/README.md` gives the build command.

## Relation to arXiv 2611.13093v1

This source is the authors' revised version, prepared on 2026-11-11 for the second arXiv version. It differs from arXiv v1 only by the minor corrections below. The section numbers are the same in both versions.

1. **Missing reference.** The bibliography now contains `Pal:2020wwd` (Pal and Sun, JHEP 08 (2020) 064), which was missing from the v1 upload. In v1 the footnote to Sec. 2.2 that cites it ("For a group, the Cardy growth ... was first derived in") shows `[?]`.
2. **Sec. 2.5.2.** The level-by-level coincidences hold "to within $7\times10^{-14}$". v1 had $2\times10^{-14}$, below the measured $6.5\times10^{-14}$ of one $(\mathbf 1,\tau)$, $(\tau,\mathbf 1)$ pair.
3. **Sec. 2.4.3.** A new sentence states that Table 1 and Figs. 3 and 4 use one set of 120 disorder realizations, and Figs. 5 and 6 a second, independent set. v1 described a single set.
4. **Footnote to Table 1.** The gap ratios lie below the RMT benchmarks "by 1.5 to 5.3 standard deviations". v1 had 5.7, computed from the rounded table entries.
5. **Typos.**
   - Footnote in Sec. 2.5: "previously mentioned", and a missing space after "$\mathrm{Rep}(S_3)$."
   - Sec. 4.1: a stray bracket after $[J_m,J_n]=2km\,\delta_{m+n,0}$.
   - Sec. 4.3: "arise".
   - Sec. 4.4.2: "receives", the punctuation after $D_{\mathbf m}=d_{m_L}d_{m_R}$, "The last two equations arise because $|k\rangle$ and $|-k\rangle$ lie in the same", and the missing square in $\mathsf{CRT}_{\bar V}^2=(-1)^k$.
   - Sec. 5.1: $SL(2,\mathbb Z)$ for $SL(2;\mathbb Z)$.
   - Sec. 6: "global".
